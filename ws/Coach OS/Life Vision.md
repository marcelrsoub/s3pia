# Life Vision

Marcel is building a life that feels calm, capable, and alive.

He is physically stronger, easier to trust, and more at home in his own body.
He communicates directly without losing warmth.
He has financial stability and senior-level frontend skill.
He ships meaningful work consistently and keeps one real project moving at a time.
He stays creative through music, performance, and small published work.
He shows up socially with presence instead of people-pleasing or disappearing.

The long-term aim is not complexity. It is grounded momentum, visible proof, and a reputation for finishing what matters.
