# Active Pillars

## 1. Body
Goal: lose weight, build fitness, improve energy, and feel more confident.
Leverage actions: gym, walking, better meals, sleep protection, no-zero body days.
Metrics: workouts, walks, weight trend, energy, sleep.

## 2. Money / Career
Goal: become financially stable and senior-frontend sharp.
Leverage actions: React/frontend practice, interview prep, job applications, architecture explanations, coding reps.
Metrics: study blocks, applications, interviews, mock answers, weak topics fixed.

## 3. Business / Building
Goal: ship useful work and avoid project scattering.
Leverage actions: one active project, small visible improvements, weekly shipping proof.
Metrics: shipped changes, user-facing improvements, demos, screenshots, commits.

## 4. Presence / Assertiveness
Goal: become more direct, respected, and socially grounded.
Leverage actions: one clean directness rep per day, fewer unnecessary apologies, less nervous joking after serious points.
Metrics: assertiveness reps, hard conversations completed, avoided conversations.

## 5. Creativity / Music
Goal: keep music alive through Yellow Magnet, recording, performing, and publishing.
Leverage actions: rehearsal, recording, song practice, publishing small outputs.
Metrics: rehearsals, recordings, posts, performances.

## 6. Relationships / Social
Goal: be good around people without people-pleasing or disappearing.
Leverage actions: direct kindness, follow-ups, presence, honest communication.
Metrics: meaningful interactions, direct conversations, social energy.
