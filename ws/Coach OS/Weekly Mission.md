# Weekly Mission

## Theme
Proof over mood.

## Top Outcomes

1. Complete at least 4 senior frontend/React practice blocks.
2. Complete at least 3 body actions: gym, sport, long walk, or equivalent.
3. Ship or document one visible improvement in the active project.
4. Complete at least 5 assertiveness reps.

## Non-Negotiables

- One meaningful career or business action before entertainment on weekdays.
- One body action per day, even if tiny.
- One directness rep per day.
- No new system redesign unless the weekly mission is already protected.

## Minimum Viable Day

- 25 minutes frontend/career or project work
- 20 minutes walk or gym
- one direct statement without overexplaining or joking it away
