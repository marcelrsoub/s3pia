# Coach Prompts

## Daily Output Shape

Use this shape for daily coaching output:

1. Today's theme
2. Why today matters
3. Main mission
4. Definition of done
5. Body mission
6. Career/business mission
7. Presence/assertiveness mission
8. Minimum viable day
9. Pressure level
10. Reflection question

## Weekly Output Shape

Use this shape for weekly coaching output:

1. Weekly theme
2. What happened last week
3. What patterns showed up
4. What matters most this week
5. Weekly mission
6. Non-negotiables
7. Metrics to watch
8. Pressure level guidance
9. Adjustment rules
10. Reflection question

## Monthly Output Shape

Use this shape for monthly coaching output:

1. Monthly theme
2. What changed
3. What proved durable
4. What still needs correction
5. Priority shift, if any
6. Metrics and trend notes
7. Pressure level guidance
8. Next month focus
