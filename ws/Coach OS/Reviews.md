# Reviews

## Weekly Review Template

- What got done this week?
- What was avoided?
- What proof changed the story?
- What patterns showed up?
- What is the smallest next correction?
- What should stay the same next week?

## Monthly Review Template

- What changed over the month?
- Which habits became real?
- Which promises were kept or broken repeatedly?
- What leverage is working?
- What needs to be removed, simplified, or protected?
- What is the next month's focus?
